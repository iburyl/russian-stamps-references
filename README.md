# Russian Stamps References

Contents and reference index for journals and other publications related to Russian philately.

The repository is intended as a research aid for locating articles on Russian postal history, postage stamps, postal stationery, revenues, printing methods, varieties, and related subjects.

The main focus is on publications that are otherwise difficult to search systematically. Journal contents are transcribed into structured Markdown tables with issue numbers, page ranges, article titles, authors, and additional notes where available.

## Journal contents

### Rossica Journal

Complete contents index for **Rossica — Journal of the Rossica Society of Russian Philately**.

Issues: [1–186](rossica_contents.md)

The index covers both Russian- and English-language sections of the journal and is intended to make articles searchable across the complete run.

### Марки

Contents index for the pre-revolutionary Russian philatelic journal **«Марки»**, including separate references to Russian stamp issues and zemstvo districts mentioned in each issue.

Годы: [1896–1901](marki_contents.md)

### Марки и коллекционер

Contents index for the first Russian-German philatelic journal **«Марки и коллекционер»**, including its advertising prospectus, trial issue, regular issues, and separate references to Russian postal issues.

Годы: [1903–1910](marki_i_kollektsioner_contents.md)

### Филателия (Санкт-Петербург — Петроград)

Contents index for the pre-revolutionary illustrated stamp journal **«Филателия»**, including the trial issue of 1913, the surviving issues for 1914–1915, and the publisher's separate price list.

Годы: [1913–1915](filateliya_spb_contents.md)

### Советский коллекционер

Contents index for the philatelic and collecting almanac **«Советский коллекционер»**.

Выпуски: [1–28](soviet_collector.md)

### Филателия СССР

Verified contents index for **«Филателия СССР»**, transcribed from the
printed contents pages and arranged in reverse chronological order.

Годы: [1966–1969](filateliya_sssr_contents.md)

## Scope and accuracy

The repository is primarily a **bibliographic and research index**, not a catalogue of stamps.

Article titles, authors, pagination, and other bibliographic information are transcribed from the original publications where possible.

The indexes are being expanded and corrected as additional material is reviewed.
